## 摘要
Mindspeller 从三种来源生成神经画像：理性自我报告、基于联想的语义定位，以及认知任务期间记录的表现和 EEG。任务与 EEG 数据流是唯一用于创建职业证据的来源。只有当参与者的任务表现支持预期构念，且相应的 EEG 数据通过所需的质量与证据检查后，结果才能进入角色匹配。当前试点使用四个 EEG 电极、十二项计分任务、23 项 O*NET 能力以及包含 376 种职业的内部库。自我报告与关联证据有助于解释动机、偏好和契合度，但不会生成角色。该输出旨在支持关于认知匹配的讨论。它不是招聘决策，不是实际工作技能的测量，也不是工作绩效的预测。角色置信度目前上限为“中等”，外部心理测量效度与工作结果效度尚未确立。

## Abstract
Mindspeller produces a Neuroprofile from three sources: rational self-report, association-based semantic positioning, and performance recorded during cognitive tasks together with EEG. The task-and-EEG stream is the only source used to create occupational evidence. A result can enter role matching only after the participant's task performance supports the intended construct and the corresponding EEG data pass the required quality and evidence checks. The current pilot uses four EEG electrodes, twelve scored tasks, 23 O*NET abilities, and an internal bank of 376 occupations. Self-report and association evidence help explain motivation, preference, and alignment, but do not generate roles. The output is intended to support discussion about cognitive fit. It is not a hiring decision, a measure of practical job skill, or a prediction of job performance. Role confidence is currently capped at Moderate, and external psychometric and job-outcome validity have not yet been established.
